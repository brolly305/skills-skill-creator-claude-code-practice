Before you call

Quick checks that
might save a visit.

Sometimes it's a setting or a breaker. If these don't fix it, call or text and we'll take it from there.

A/C is running but not cooling
Make sure the thermostat is set to Cool and below the room temperature. Replace the air filter if it's dirty. Check that the outdoor unit's breaker is on and nothing is blocking the unit. If you see ice on the lines or the indoor coil, turn the system off and call us.
No heat from the furnace
Check that the thermostat is set to Heat, and replace its batteries if the screen is blank. Make sure the furnace power switch (it looks like a light switch, on or near the furnace) is on, and the furnace door is closed tight. A badly clogged filter can also shut a furnace down.
No hot water, or the water heater is leaking
For a leak, close the cold-water valve on top of the heater and turn off its power or gas, then call. With no hot water, an electric heater may have a tripped breaker; on a gas heater the pilot may be out. Follow the instructions on the tank's label.
I smell gas
Leave the house right away. Don't turn lights or appliances on or off, and don't use your phone inside. Once you're outside, call your gas company's emergency line or 911.
