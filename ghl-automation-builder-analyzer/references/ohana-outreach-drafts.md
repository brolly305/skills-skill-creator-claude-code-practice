## Follow-Up Schedule (A2 / B2)

If no reply after 5 business days, send a one-sentence bump:

**Attorney follow-up:** `Hey [FirstName] — just bumping this up in case it got buried. Happy to jump on a 10-minute call if easier.`

**Realtor follow-up:** `Hey [FirstName] — still happy to get on your vendor list whenever the timing works.`

After 10 business days with no response, move contact to `partner-cold` tag in GHL and enroll in 90-day reactivation sequence.

## GHL Tags to Apply on Reply

| Event | Tag |
|-------|-----|
| Reply received | `partner-responded` |
| Call booked | `partner-call-scheduled` |
| Referral agreement signed | `partner-active` |
| No reply after 10 days | `partner-cold` |

Pipeline stage on first reply: move to **Relationship Building** stage in GHL Referral Partner pipeline.
