# Template: First Outreach Message

Use this for warm introductions — businesses you've been referred to or that someone in your network knows.

---

## Version A — Text / DM (Short)

```
Hey [Name], I'm Hansel — I run HOL and also co-own Ohana Property & Transition Services here in Lafayette.

Quick question: when someone Googles "[their business name]", what do they find?

I ask because I've been helping local businesses that do great work but are basically invisible online. Figured I'd reach out directly. No pitch — just a real conversation if it's relevant to you.
```

---

## Version B — In-Person Opening Line

```
"Do you ever get customers who found you on Google?"

[If no/rarely:]
"That's actually why I wanted to talk to you. You've built something real here — the community knows it. The problem is Google doesn't. I fix that for businesses like yours."
```

---

## Version C — Follow-Up After No Response (Day 7)

```
Hey [Name] — following up on my last message.

I looked up [Business Name] on Google and noticed [specific thing: no profile / few reviews / outdated info]. 

That's fixable. If it's not the right time, no problem. But if you're curious what that change actually looks like, I'm happy to show you what I did for my own company first.
```

---

## Fill-In Checklist Before Sending
- [ ] Used their actual first name
- [ ] Referenced something specific about their business (not generic)
- [ ] Didn't use the word "services," "solutions," or "leverage"
- [ ] Under 75 words (for texts/DMs)
- [ ] No exclamation points beyond one
