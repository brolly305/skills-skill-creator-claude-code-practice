# Template: Client Onboarding Checklist

Complete this for every new HOL client. Target: under 4 hours total.

---

## Before You Start (30 min)
- [ ] Get access to their Google Business Profile (request ownership or manager access)
- [ ] Get 3–5 photos from the client (their team, their work, their location)
- [ ] Collect: business hours, service area, top 3 services, phone number, website (if any)
- [ ] Ask: "Who is your best customer? Describe them in a sentence."

## Google Business Profile Setup (1–1.5 hrs)
- [ ] Claim or verify ownership of the profile
- [ ] Complete every field: name, category, description, hours, phone, website, service area
- [ ] Write AI-generated business description (use voice-profile.md tone — peer-to-peer, no corporate speak)
- [ ] Add all services with descriptions and prices (ranges are fine)
- [ ] Upload 5–10 photos
- [ ] Set up Q&A section with 3 common questions and answers
- [ ] Verify the profile is live and accurate on Google Maps

## Review Collection System Setup (30–45 min)
- [ ] Create their review request text message template (short, personal, includes direct Google review link)
- [ ] Walk the client through how to send it (after every completed job, within 24 hours)
- [ ] Send a test request to yourself to confirm the link works
- [ ] Set a reminder: check review count at the 30-day mark

## First Month Content Queue (1–1.5 hrs)
- [ ] Write 4 Google Business Profile posts using AI (one per week)
  - Post 1: Who you are / what you do
  - Post 2: A recent job or project (before/after if possible)
  - Post 3: A community connection or local mention
  - Post 4: A common question you answer + call to action
- [ ] Schedule or send posts with dates noted
- [ ] Set up AI auto-response for Google messages (or draft a template the client can copy-paste)

## Client Handoff (15 min)
- [ ] Send a simple summary: what was done, what to expect in 30 days, what they need to do (send review requests after jobs)
- [ ] Confirm the best way to reach them monthly (text, email, call)
- [ ] Log client info in PROJECTS folder

---

## What to Track Month-Over-Month
- Google profile views
- Search appearances
- Review count and average rating
- Calls / direction requests from Google
- Any new customers who mention finding them online
