# System Prompt: Monthly Client Report (Level 2 Recipe)

**Workflow:** Generate a performance update to send to each HOL client at month-end
**Trigger:** End of each calendar month, per active client

---

## The Prompt (paste this, fill in brackets)

```
Read my HOL project files: about-me.md, voice-profile.md, anti-ai-writing-style.md.

Write a monthly performance report for [CLIENT NAME] — [BUSINESS NAME].

This month's numbers:
- Google reviews: [start count] → [end count] (+[X] new)
- Average rating: [X.X stars]
- Profile views: [number]
- Search appearances: [number]
- Calls from Google: [number]
- Direction requests: [number]
- New customers who cited Google: [number or "unknown"]

Notable wins this month: [anything specific — a strong review, a big job that came from Google, a post that performed well]

Issues or flags: [anything not working, any complaint, any gap — or "none"]

Rules:
- Keep it under 200 words total
- Lead with the wins, not the numbers
- Translate numbers into plain language ("your profile was seen 400 times — that's 400 people who could have called you")
- End with one thing they should do this month (usually: keep sending review requests after jobs)
- Tone: direct, encouraging, peer-to-peer — not corporate reporting language
- Format for text or email — not a formal document
```

---

## Delivery
Send via the client's preferred channel (text or email) in the first week of the following month.
Log the report in their PROJECTS file.
