# System Prompt: Write Outreach Message (Level 2 Recipe)

**Workflow:** First contact with a local business prospect
**Trigger:** You've identified a business that fits the HOL target profile

---

## The Prompt (paste this, fill in brackets)

```
Read my HOL project files: about-me.md, voice-profile.md, anti-ai-writing-style.md.

Write a first outreach message to [BUSINESS NAME], a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] in [CITY].
Context about them: [1-2 sentences — what you know: how long they've been around, what they do, how you found them, mutual connection if any].
Channel: [text / DM / email / in-person opener].

Rules:
- Peer-to-peer tone — I'm a fellow business owner, not a marketer
- Under 75 words for text/DM, under 150 for email
- Must reference something specific about their business
- End with one low-commitment question, not a pitch
- No exclamation points beyond one
- Do not mention "AI," "digital marketing," or "services" in the first message

Write 2 versions. Label them A and B. I will pick one.
```

---

## What Good Output Looks Like

**Good:** Specific, short, sounds like a text from a real person, ends with a genuine question.

**Bad:** Starts with "I hope this finds you well," mentions "leveraging digital tools," has 3 bullet points, ends with "Let me know if you'd like to schedule a call."
