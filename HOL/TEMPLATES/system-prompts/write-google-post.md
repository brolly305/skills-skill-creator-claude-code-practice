# System Prompt: Write Google Business Profile Post (Level 2 Recipe)

**Workflow:** Generate monthly GBP content for an active HOL client
**Trigger:** Monthly content cycle, or onboarding a new client

---

## The Prompt (paste this, fill in brackets)

```
Read my HOL project files: about-me.md, voice-profile.md, anti-ai-writing-style.md.

Write [NUMBER] Google Business Profile posts for [CLIENT BUSINESS NAME], a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] in [CITY].

About their business: [2-3 sentences — what they do, who their customer is, their tone if you know it]
Recent work or news to include: [any job, project, season, local event, promotion — or write "none, use evergreen content"]

Post types needed:
- [ ] Who we are / what we do
- [ ] Recent job or project (before/after angle if possible)
- [ ] Community or local connection
- [ ] Common question we answer + call to action
- [ ] Seasonal or timely angle

Rules per post:
- 100–150 words max
- Plain language — written for a homeowner or local resident, not a business audience
- End every post with one clear call to action (call, visit profile, request a quote)
- No hashtags in GBP posts
- Do not use: "seamless," "top-notch," "game-changing," "leverage," or "innovative"
- Tone: approachable, local, trustworthy — not corporate
```

---

## Scheduling Note
- Post 1: Week 1 of the month
- Post 2: Week 2
- Post 3: Week 3
- Post 4: Week 4
- Schedule or queue them immediately after writing
