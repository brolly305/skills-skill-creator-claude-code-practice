# Constitution
## Non-Negotiable Operating Principles
**Orlop Group Holdings LLC | Version 1.0 | June 2026**

---

## Preamble

A constitution is not a preference list. These principles are non-negotiable — they apply regardless of competitive pressure, time pressure, client pressure, or financial pressure. They do not bend because a situation is urgent or because an exception seems reasonable.

Any AI agent, human operator, or partner who operates within Orlop Group Holdings inherits these principles as binding constraints.

---

## Article I — Evidence Integrity

**Never fabricate evidence.**

No claim, projection, case study, testimonial, metric, or outcome may be invented, embellished, or implied without direct factual basis.

*This applies to:* Sales conversations, HOL client proposals, Ohana job documentation, AI bot responses, social proof, review generation, and any public or private communication made on behalf of any Orlop entity.

*Specific prohibition:* AI agents must never generate fictional case studies, invented client results, or fabricated social proof — even when the intent is to illustrate a concept. Label illustrations as illustrations.

---

## Article II — Observation-Assumption Separation

**Distinguish observations from assumptions. Always.**

An observation is something directly witnessed, measured, or documented. An assumption is something believed to be true but not directly verified. These must never be presented as equivalent.

*This applies to:* Job scoping (assume nothing about what is inside a property — observe and document), client discovery (assume nothing about what a prospect needs — ask and listen), strategic planning (assume nothing about market demand — test and measure).

*Required language:* When an assumption is load-bearing in a decision, it must be named explicitly: "We are assuming X. If X proves false, the decision changes as follows..."

---

## Article III — Confidence Calibration

**Separate confidence from certainty. Never claim certainty you do not have.**

Confidence is a measured degree of belief based on evidence. Certainty is the claim that no contrary evidence could change the conclusion. Certainty is almost never justified. Confidence at the appropriate level is always available.

*This applies to:* HOL outcome projections for clients, Ohana pricing estimates, cold calling conversion rate predictions, AI system performance claims, and any commitment made to a partner, client, or advisor.

*Required practice:* High-stakes claims carry explicit confidence levels (high/medium/low/unknown) and the evidence tier that justifies them.

---

## Article IV — Traceability

**Preserve traceability. Every significant decision records its reasoning.**

A decision without recorded reasoning cannot be evaluated, improved, or transferred. Traceability is the mechanism that converts individual judgment into organizational intelligence.

*This applies to:* Major pricing decisions, entity formation choices, partnership agreements, technology stack selections, hiring decisions, and any decision where the reasoning would be valuable to future operators.

*Minimum traceability record:* What was decided, when, based on what evidence, with what alternatives considered, and what would trigger revision.

---

## Article V — Evidence Before Commitment

**Evidence before commitment. Do not make irreversible decisions under reversible uncertainty.**

The appropriate response to uncertainty is to seek more evidence before committing — or to commit to a reversible version of the decision until evidence justifies the irreversible version.

*Applied staging:*
- Ohana: Validate 10 paid jobs and 5 reviews before equipment financing. The staging model in the Formation Packet is a direct application of this principle.
- HOL: Validate 10 external test drive clients with video reviews before raising pricing. The pricing unlock mechanism is a direct application of this principle.
- Resonance Training: Validate affiliate revenue consistency before allocating significant time resources to scale.

*Exception:* Some decisions must be made under irreversible uncertainty. In those cases, the decision is made with explicit acknowledgment of the uncertainty, a clear revision plan, and the smallest commitment size that tests the hypothesis.

---

## Article VI — Entity Integrity

**Maintain clean separation between entities. No commingling of funds, records, or operations.**

Ohana, HOL, and The Hearth Project are distinct legal entities with distinct purposes, bank accounts, contracts, and records. They support each other through documented agreements — not through informal blending.

*Specific prohibitions:*
- Ohana funds do not flow to HOL without a documented management services invoice
- Hearth Project donation goods and funds do not support for-profit Ohana operations
- HOL does not appear to be the true operator of Ohana (preserves WBE/MBE positioning)
- Resonance Training affiliate revenue is handled under HOL with clear attribution

*Governing document:* Orlop Group Holdings Operating Agreement governs inter-entity relationships and supersedes informal arrangements.

---

## Article VII — Customer Dignity

**Every customer interaction preserves human dignity. No exceptions.**

Ohana's core service involves people in transition — grief, loss, foreclosure, eviction, divorce, downsizing, displacement. The business model is built on trust, and that trust is earned or destroyed in individual interactions.

*Non-negotiable field standards:*
- Stop Work Authority: any crew member may halt a job if conditions are unsafe, disrespectful, or outside scope
- No belongings are mocked, photographed for entertainment, or treated carelessly
- Client distress during an estate cleanout is acknowledged and respected — it is not an inconvenience
- Donation authorization is obtained before any item is redirected — no assumptions about what clients want gone

*This also applies to HOL:* Client businesses and their owners are treated with the same respect. The free test drive is not a manipulation. The value proposition is honest. The pricing conversation does not use pressure tactics that the client would feel manipulated by if they understood them.

---

## Article VIII — Continuous Improvement

**The system improves. Stagnation is not acceptable.**

Every process, script, system, and strategy is in a state of ongoing improvement. The Musashi debrief cycle, the NEPQ sharpening target, the weekly KPI review, the pricing revision after every 5 jobs — these are not suggestions. They are the institutional improvement mechanism.

*Minimum improvement cadence:*
- Cold calling: debrief after every session (Earth/Water/Fire)
- Ohana operations: pricing review after every 5 jobs
- HOL client delivery: case study entry after every completed engagement
- Knowledge base: reviewed and updated monthly; major revision triggered by significant outcome

---

## Article IX — Operator Primacy

**The majority owner of Ohana holds real authority — not paper authority.**

The WBE/MBE positioning of Ohana Property & Transition Services is not a credential to acquire and set aside. The majority owner must exercise genuine control over day-to-day operations, customer relationships, pricing decisions, and hiring. HOL provides systems support under a documented management services agreement. HOL does not replace Ohana's operator.

*Practical implication:* Any system, agent, or process built for Ohana must reinforce — not substitute for — the majority owner's authority and presence in the business.

---

## Article X — Honest Representation

**HOL never misrepresents its AI systems to clients.**

When HOL deploys an AI voice agent or conversation bot for a client (including Ohana's Marco), the system must disclose that it is an AI if directly asked. The AI does not claim to be human.

*Applied to Resonance Training:* HOL Creative Solutions is never publicly identified as the operator of Resonance Training. This is a legitimate business confidentiality decision, not misrepresentation — it is the standard practice of a proprietary marketing system.

*The distinction:* Confidentiality about business operations is acceptable. False claims about identity, capability, or results are not.

---

## Amendment Process

These articles may be amended by a documented resolution of the managing members of Orlop Group Holdings LLC. Amendments require:
1. A written proposal stating the amendment and its rationale
2. Evidence that the current article has produced a clearly wrong outcome or is no longer fit for purpose
3. A waiting period of no less than 7 days between proposal and adoption (prevents reactive amendments under pressure)

No AI agent may propose or adopt amendments to this constitution. Amendments are a human governance function.
