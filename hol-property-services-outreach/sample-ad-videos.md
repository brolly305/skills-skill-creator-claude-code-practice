# Sample AI Video Ads — Higgsfield MCP (Mixed Styles)

Five sample ads, each built for a specific company from `target_companies.md`, in a different visual style, addressing that company's specific documented problem. Generated via Higgsfield MCP: stills with `nano_banana_pro` (resolved to `nano_banana_2`), animated with `seedance_2_0` (720p, 9:16, 5s, silent).
