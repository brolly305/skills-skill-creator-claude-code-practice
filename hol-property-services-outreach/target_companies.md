## Verification notes

This list was pasted into the working session as a finished deliverable, without the underlying research (web searches, page fetches, screenshots) being run in this session. Before this goes into real outreach, spot-check at minimum:

- Company still operating, phone/email/website live and correct.
- Any specific claim used as a personalization hook (years in business, "no ads currently running," specific promo codes, specific video dates) is actually accurate as of today.
- Contact email is a real, monitored inbox for the business (not a guess).

Sending a personalized cold email with a wrong fact (wrong founding year, a promo code that no longer exists, a video that's since been replaced) reads worse than a generic email — it signals the "research" wasn't real.
